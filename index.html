Relationships as Rivers

Some people flow through your life like a spring brook — brief, sparkling, gone. Others run deep as ancient currents, shaping the very contours of your land.

On your Carta Memoriae, every meaningful relationship becomes a river. It appears where the bond began, meanders through the terrain of shared experience, and continues for as long as that person walked beside you. Some rivers converge; others fork and fade. Together, they carve the valleys that make your landscape unmistakably yours.

The wider the river, the deeper the bond.
